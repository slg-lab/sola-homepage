RoboDesign1M introduces a large-scale dataset and foundation-model benchmark for robotic mechanism and design understanding.

The project focuses on multimodal robotic design data at scale, supporting downstream tasks such as retrieval, generation, and broader AI-assisted robot design workflows.

Paper: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06796)
