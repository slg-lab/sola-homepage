A workspace-oriented design optimization framework for multi-section continuum robots. Sparse point propagation (SPP) efficiently estimates the workspace under a piecewise constant curvature model, while NSGA-II optimizes the number of sections, section lengths, and maximum bending angles to balance workspace area, total robot length, and total bending angle.
